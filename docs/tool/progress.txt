Track progress through the commit-summary analysis workflow.

Use this tool before and after each major step in the commit prompt. It returns
a one-line summary of the updated step and overall progress.

Rules:
- Update exactly one step per call.
- Mark a step `in_progress` before you do that step's work.
- Mark the same step `completed` only after the work is done.
- `note` is required when `status` is `completed`; include the evidence or result.
- If a step does not apply (e.g. Step 5 for small diffs), mark it `completed` with
  a note explaining why it was skipped.
- Do not mark several steps complete at once.

Steps:
- Step 1 — Load context
- Step 2 — Survey the diff
- Step 3 — Classify and group
- Step 4 — Contextual deep-dive
- Step 5 — Subagent exploration
- Step 6 — Verify the summary
- Step 7 — Write the summary

Example — mark a step in progress:
```json
{
  "step": "Step 2 — Survey the diff",
  "status": "in_progress"
}
```
Returns: `Step 2 — Survey the diff in_progress. 1/7 done.`

Example — complete a step with evidence:
```json
{
  "step": "Step 2 — Survey the diff",
  "status": "completed",
  "note": "4 files changed, 80 added, 12 deleted"
}
```
Returns: `Step 2 — Survey the diff completed (4 files changed, 80 added, 12 deleted). 2/7 done.`

Example — skip a step that does not apply:
```json
{
  "step": "Step 5 — Subagent exploration",
  "status": "completed",
  "note": "skipped: small diff, no subagent needed"
}
```
Returns: `Step 5 — Subagent exploration completed (skipped: small diff, no subagent needed). 5/7 done.`
